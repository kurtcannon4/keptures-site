Keptures App Keptures turns "I'll do it later" into something you can't casually skip. Add a task, set a due date, and that due date locks — no dragging it out, no quiet rescheduling. If life happens and you truly need more time, you can unlock it with credits you've earned, not credits you bought. Finish something? You need a quick photo as proof — a shot of the finished task, or just a smile — before it counts as done. Every task you add and every task you finish earns credits. Spend them on due-date overrides when you genuinely need one, or save them for your companion creature — pick a starter shape, then unlock new colors, hats, glasses, and more as you go. Overdue tasks don't just vanish either: they stay visible until you deal with them, with an occasional built-in "amnesty" if you need a clean slate. Features: Due dates that lock once set — override them with earned credits, not shortcuts Photo proof required to mark a task complete Earn credits by adding tasks and by finishing them (with a daily cap so it can't be gamed) A companion creature that levels up its look as you unlock colors, accessories, and even new base shapes Themed lists to organize tasks however makes sense to you An Overdue view that keeps missed tasks visible instead of letting them disappear Archive finished tasks to keep your lists clean without deleting your history Light and dark mode All your data syncs privately through your own iCloud account Keptures was built for anyone with a running list of good intentions who wants a little external structure to actually follow through — including people who find that a due date without real stakes just doesn't stick. No ads. No tracking. No accounts to create — just your iCloud. Contact: kurtc14+keptures@gmail.com
